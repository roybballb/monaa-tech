Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI detection mechanics and offline runtime.

How does Turnitin AI detection score academic papers?

Turnitin uses statistical language models to calculate the cross-entropy log-probability of each token (perplexity) alongside sentence length variation (burstiness). Because formal academic papers adhere to rigid vocabulary and structured grammar, human research dissertations are frequently flagged as synthetic false positives.

Does my document leave my computer when using MONAA Paraphrase-R?

Never. MONAA Paraphrase-R runs entirely on your local machine using standard Python 3.8+. It requires zero internet connection to execute, meaning your unpublished research, confidential legal briefs, or dissertations are never exposed to external cloud servers or training databases.

What is the difference between Paraphrase-R Lite and Full?

Paraphrase-R Lite ($29) is an evaluation tier allowing you to run a single document up to 5,000 words through the full adversarial engine. Paraphrase-R Full ($149) grants unlimited lifetime documents, multi-pass Best-of-N candidate search, citation slot preservation, and priority support.

How does the 100% Citation Shielding work?

Before performing any syntactic transformation, our tokenizer detects statutory legal citations, bibliography indices (e.g. [12], (Smith et al., 2024)), and LaTeX math equations, substituting them with cryptographic slot anchors. Once cadence optimization is complete, the anchors are restored character-for-character.
